About

EventFlow AI

EventFlow AI was created by Bilal Siddiqui, a developer who loves building tools that make people's work easier and more impactful.

The idea behind EventFlow AI is simple: organizing an event involves a surprising amount of repetitive, time-consuming work — managing workflows, preparing and distributing certificates, and keeping participants engaged. Bilal built EventFlow AI to operate and automate that entire lifecycle in one place:

  • Events — plan and manage your event operations from a single workspace.
  • Certificates — import attendees, generate personalized certificates, and distribute them by email automatically.
  • Quizzes — run interactive, AI-generated quiz sessions with live results, so participants stay engaged and learning is fun.

The goal was to add real value to the work and efforts of every organizer and participant who uses it — so they can focus on what matters most instead of the busywork.
